# Adhan Alarm – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 24 October 2026*

Adhan Alarm ("the app") is a prayer-times and adhan app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **the app collects nothing, shares nothing, and has no internet access.**

## Data the app uses

**Location.** If you tap "Use my current location", the app reads your approximate location once to calculate prayer times. The coordinates are stored only on your phone. You can instead type a city or coordinates; location permission is optional.

**City names.** To show a city name or search for a city, the app asks your phone's built-in Android location service (Geocoder). That lookup is handled by your phone's system services, not by the app, under your phone provider's own privacy terms.

**Settings and sounds.** Your settings and any adhan audio file you choose are stored only inside the app on your phone.

## What the app does not do

- It does not request the Internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
- It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- It does not sell or share any data with anyone.

## Permissions

- **Location (optional):** calculate prayer times for where you are.
- **Notifications, alarms, full-screen alerts:** play the adhan on time and show the Stop button.
- **Foreground media playback, wake lock, vibration:** play the adhan while the phone is locked.
- **Run at startup:** re-schedule the next adhan after a reboot.
- **Battery optimisation exemption (optional):** stop Android from delaying the adhan.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/aminebeg/adhan-alarm/issues>.

# Politique de confidentialité

L'application ne collecte et ne partage aucune donnée, et n'a pas accès à internet. La position est utilisée une seule fois, sur votre téléphone, pour calculer les heures de prière. Aucune publicité, aucun pistage. Désinstaller l'application supprime toutes ses données.
